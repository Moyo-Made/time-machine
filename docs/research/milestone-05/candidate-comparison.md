# Ranked comparison of the three candidates

The three candidate locations from the Milestone 05 brief, ranked. The scores are from Checkpoint 1; the ranking was then tested against the evidence gates in Checkpoint 2. **The gates decided the outcome, not the scores.**

| Rank | Candidate | Outcome |
|---|---|---|

| 3 | A bounded section of Broad Street | Not pursued |

## What looking at the evidence did to each

### 1. Tinubu Square

- **The whole square failed.** Around 1950, only two buildings have a complete front on record. Most of the scene would have been invented.
- **One plot passed.** The Casa do Fernandez has dated photographs from the summer of 2016, a dated record of its plot one week after the demolition, a position fixed on survey plans, and a careful archival study.
- **The scope shrank to what passed**: one building, the ground in front of it, and two dates ten weeks apart.

Evidence for the selected scope is stronger than the Checkpoint 1 score of 3 suggested, and for the square as a whole it is weaker.

### 2. The Marina

- **Its advantage was dated photographs.** On inspection, two of the "1947" items are photographs of framed drawings, and the other dated views are distant or aerial.
- **Its good street-level fronts are undated.**
- **Its size is still the obstacle**: a long waterfront that one developer cannot evidence building by building.
- I viewed 8 of 85 catalogued photographs. That is a sample, not an assessment.

It stays the fallback because it has named, surviving landmarks and the richest archive. It would need the same gate review before use.

### 3. Broad Street

- 24 of 25 catalogued photographs are undated, and many show walls with posters, not buildings.
- I viewed 1 of the 25.
- Nothing found in Checkpoint 2 improved its position.

## Why the selected scope is the right size

| | Selected scope | Whole square | Marina section |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buildings to reconstruct | 1 | 6 or more | 5 or 6 |
| Buildings with a complete front on record | 1 | 2 | Not established |
| Periods dated to the day | 2 | 0 | Not established |
| Share of the scene that would be conjecture | Low for the plot; the surroundings are placeholders | Most of it | Not established |

A small scene that can be defended is worth more to this project than a large one that cannot.
