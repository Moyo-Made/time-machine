# Source inventory

Every source used in Checkpoint 2, with its address and what I actually did with it. Accessed 10 October 2026.

## Access status

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Viewed** | I retrieved the image or map and looked at it |
| **Read** | I retrieved the record or text and read it |
| **Reported** | I know it only through another source; not verified |
| **Blocked** | I could not reach it |

## Kinds of source

- **Primary:** made at the time, by someone who was there. Photographs, survey plans.
- **Secondary:** a later study or description. The thesis; an archivist's catalogue note.
- **Tertiary and press:** summaries and news reports. Leads, not evidence.

## Not found

- A survey plan at a scale large enough to show the building's footprint.
- **Any dated photograph of the building's plot later than February 2021.** Two web searches and the earlier search of Wikimedia Commons found nothing from 2022 to 2026.
- Any freely licensed photograph of the plot after the demolition. The two sets I found, from 18 September 2016 (W-2016) and February 2021 (T-2021), are both in copyright.
- Any source for the claim that the square's fountain was a gift from the Lebanese community.
- The 1956 gazette notice itself, and the 2012 measured drawings at a usable size. The thesis confirms both exist.
