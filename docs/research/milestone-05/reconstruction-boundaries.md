# Reconstruction boundaries and unknowns

What a reconstruction of the selected site may contain, what it must label as conjecture, and what it must leave out. This is the contract for Milestone 06 and for any model made later.

**Selected on 10 October 2026:** the Casa do Fernandez (Ilojo Bar, Olaiya House) plot and its frontage, at two dates.

| Period | Label to use | Dated by |
|---|---|---|
| A | Summer 2016 (17 July to 7 September) | Camera dates on five photographs |
| B | 18 September 2016 | Ten photographs published that day with an eyewitness account |

References such as C-12 and W-2016 are listed in [source-inventory.md](source-inventory.md).

## The boundary on the ground

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inside** | The building's plot at the corner of Alli Street and Bamgbose Street, and the strip of ground between its front and the edge of Tinubu Square |
| **Edge** | The player can walk the frontage and along the Bamgbose Street side. The limit is a few metres beyond the plot in each direction |
| **Outside** | Tinubu Square itself, the streets beyond the corner, and every other building. These appear only as plain placeholder masses, marked as not reconstructed |

The player cannot enter the building. No interior is evidenced beyond one stair and one partial view.

## Three levels, and how each must appear

| Level | Meaning | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidenced** | Seen in a dated image, or on a dated map, that is cited | Built as seen, with its sources attached |
| **Estimated** | Its existence is evidenced; its size or position is my estimate | Built, and marked as estimated, with the assumption stated |
| **Placeholder** | Something stood there; what it looked like is not evidenced | A plain neutral mass, marked as not reconstructed |

Nothing may appear without one of these three marks. The player must be able to see which is which.

## What must be left out

| Left out | Why |
|---|---|
| **People who can be identified** | The photographs show officials, residents and passers-by. None is to be depicted or named |
| **Who was responsible for the demolition, and why** | Disputed. The game names no cause and no person |
| **The demolition itself** | Not reconstructed. The game shows two states of the plot, not the event |
| **The archival photographs and the thesis's pictures** | In copyright. They inform the model; they are not shown |
| **A construction year** | The evidence gives a range, by inference |
| **Any claim connecting the building with the slave trade** | Rejected for this building by the only archival study |
| **The interior** | Not evidenced |
| **Lettering I could not read** | Banners and the notice are shown as blank shapes, not with invented words |
| **The plot today** | No dated evidence later than February 2021 |
| **The mid-century period** | Deferred until its two dated photographs are seen in the original |

## Unknowns that a player should be told

These are part of the content, not gaps to hide.

1. The building's exact dimensions. It was measured in 2012; this reconstruction did not use those drawings.
2. What the rear looked like.
3. What the banners and the notice said.
4. Who occupied the building in 2016.
5. What the plot looked like between 18 September 2016 and February 2021, and what it looks like now.
6. When the building was made. The best account is between 1903 and 1914.

## How this can be checked

- Every evidenced element cites a reference that resolves to an address in [source-inventory.md](source-inventory.md).
- Every estimated element states its assumption in the table above.
- Milestone 06 turns these rules into automated checks; see [data-model-proposal.md](data-model-proposal.md).
