Research for choosing the first real Lagos location for Time Machine, and proposals for how to build it. No gameplay code or 3D asset was changed or made for it.

## Recommendation: conditional go, for a smaller scope than proposed

**Go ahead with the Casa do Fernandez and the ground immediately in front of it, at the north-east end of Tinubu Square. Do not go ahead with the whole square.**

| Scope and period | Result |
|---|---|

| Casa do Fernandez and its frontage, 1946 to the 1950s | **Conditional go.** Three views exist; I have seen two of them only as small reproductions |
| The whole of Tinubu Square, around 1950 | **No go.** Most of it would have to be invented |
| The plot one week after the demolition, 18 September 2016 | **Conditional go.** Ten dated photographs from one photographer, all in copyright |
| The plot as it is now | **No go yet.** Nothing dated later than February 2021 |
| A section of the Marina (fallback) | **Not needed yet; not assessed to gate standard** |

Conditions that must be met before any reconstruction starts:

1. **The building's depth, rear and heights are marked as conjecture.** Decided on 10 October 2026: the project goes ahead without the 2012 measured drawings. They exist, and could replace the estimates later if they are ever obtained.
2. **The two mid-century photographs are seen in their original publications**, not as thumbnails in a thesis.
3. **The present state of the plot is photographed and dated**, if a present-day period is wanted.
4. **The game gives no construction year.** The best account is a range, 1903 to 1914, reached by inference from land deeds.

## What looking at the evidence changed

Checkpoint 1 was built on catalogue records. For this checkpoint I looked at 34 archival photographs, 21 licensed modern photographs and three historical maps, and read the history and monument chapters of the only archival study of the building.

- **The building's position is established from maps**, not from descriptions. Survey plans of 1911 and 1913 print the name "Fernandez" at the corner where Alli Street meets the square. The 1956 monument notice, as the thesis quotes it, gives the address as 6 Alli Street and 2 Bamgbose Street. See [geography-and-periods.md](geography-and-periods.md).
- **The popular history is wrong on three points**, according to the thesis: the building dates from the early 1900s, not 1855; its owner was a merchant from Galicia in Spain, not a returnee from Brazil; and it was made by joining two houses on two plots.
- **The join is visible.** The front has three wide bays and three narrow ones, in the same proportion as the two plots' frontages on the 1914 deed. That is my inference, from a photograph and a deed sketch.
- **The early Northwestern photograph is good but undated.** Signs in it also appear in photographs of 1946 and the 1950s, which narrows it to roughly that span.
- **A verified post-demolition moment exists.** A writer visited the plot on 18 September 2016, a week after the demolition, and published ten photographs that day: fenced brick rubble, and a state signboard going up.
- **The square as a whole is thinly evidenced.** Around 1950 there is one full elevation besides the building itself.
- **Catalogue dates misled in two places.** Two "1947" Marina items are photographs of framed drawings. And the dated photographs at the square span 1948 to 1952, not one year.

## The periods

I was asked to weigh 2016 before the demolition against a verified period after it, alongside the late 1940s, and not to choose dates for the sake of a dramatic shift. Judged on evidence alone:

- **2016, before 11 September, is the best-evidenced period.** It should be the anchor.
- **18 September 2016 is the only other period dated to the day.** Paired with the anchor, it gives the firmest evidence at both ends, ten weeks apart.
- **1946 to the 1950s is usable for the building only**, labelled as a span. It is the more striking contrast and the weaker evidence.
- **The present day cannot be shown yet.**

**My recommendation is the first pairing: the building in the summer of 2016, and its plot on 18 September 2016.** The mid-century span can follow as a third period once its two dated photographs are seen in the original.

That is a decision for you, because it also decides what the game is about. The first pairing is a recent, contested event with living people involved. The game's existing rule covers it: state what is observed, keep inference weaker, name no cause and no person. The trade-offs are set out in [geography-and-periods.md](geography-and-periods.md).

## Rules followed

- **No restricted image or text is in this repository.** Preview images and the thesis PDF were read from a folder outside it. Every source is cited by address and number.
- **Northwestern's photographs are in copyright**, offered for research and scholarly use only, with no redistribution. They can inform a model. They cannot be shown in the game or stored here.
- **The thesis and the pictures in it are in copyright.** The thesis is summarised, with page numbers, not reproduced.
- **The Wikimedia Commons photographs are CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC BY 2.0.** Attribution is recorded. Anything derived directly from them would carry the share-alike condition.
- **The three maps are in the public domain.**
- **A catalogue's date span is never treated as a photograph's date.**
- **No two photographs are assumed to show the same place** unless something visible in both ties them together, and the tie is stated.
- **What the thesis reports about a document is not the document.** It is marked as reported until the document is seen.
