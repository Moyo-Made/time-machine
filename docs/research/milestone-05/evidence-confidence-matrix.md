# Evidence-confidence matrix

Each part of the scene a reconstruction would need, by period, with how well it is evidenced. References are listed in [source-inventory.md](source-inventory.md); the observations behind each row are in [visual-evidence.md](visual-evidence.md).

## Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Seen in an image or on a map that I viewed and that is dated to the period |
| **B** | Seen, but in an image whose date is uncertain, or seen only in part |
| **C** | Not seen in this period. Carried over from another period, or reasoned from what is seen |
| **D** | Reported in writing only; not verified |
| **T** | Reported by the Leiden thesis from a document it cites. I read the thesis, not the document |
| **None** | No evidence. Anything built here would be invention |

A level describes the evidence, not the likelihood. A "C" may well be true. It is still not shown.

## Reading the matrix

- **For the building in 2016, most rows are A or B.** The gaps are the footprint, the rear and the roof.
- **For the building between 1946 and the 1950s, every row is B or lower.** Nothing is A, because the one photograph I have seen properly is undated and the two dated ones I have seen only in reproduction.
- **The building's history is now mostly T.** It rests on one careful thesis and the deeds it cites. Seeing the deeds would raise it.
- **For the rest of the square, the mid-century column is mostly B, C and None.** That is why the whole-square scope fails.
- **Every row marked None must stay empty or be labelled conjecture** in anything built from this.
- **The building's footprint, depth, rear and heights stay at T or C.** The project is going ahead without the 2012 measured drawings, so these are estimates and must be shown as such.
