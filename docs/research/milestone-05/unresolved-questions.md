# Unresolved questions

What this review could not settle. Each question says what is known, why it is still open, and what would close it. References are listed in [source-inventory.md](source-inventory.md).

## Questions that block a reconstruction

### 1. When was the Casa do Fernandez built, and by whom?

**Largely answered by the thesis, which I have now read.** See [thesis-findings.md](thesis-findings.md).

| Claim | Standing |
|---|---|
| 1846, 1855, about 1880 | Traced by the thesis to one interview, an untraceable book, and an unexplained estimate |
| Two houses on two plots, the first built between 1890 and 1897; joined into one building between 1903 and 1914 | The thesis's conclusion, inferred from five land deeds and their sketch plans |
| Owner: José Amoedo Fernandez, a merchant from Galicia in Spain | Stated in the 1914 deed, as the thesis reports it |
| Builders: returnee craftsmen | The thesis's inference. The designer is undocumented |

What I can add from primary evidence I viewed:

- **Premises named "Fernandez" stood at the corner by August 1911** (M-1911). Consistent with the thesis.
- **The front has bays of two widths, in the same proportion as the two plots' frontages.** Consistent with a building made across two plots.

**Still open:**

- **The year.** The deeds bracket it; nothing states it.
- **Whether the joining began before 1903.** The thesis says this may never be known.
- **I have not seen the deeds.** Everything rests on one researcher's reading of them.

**What would close it:** the five deeds at the Lagos Land Registry; building or rating records, if any survive.

**For the game:** no construction year. A range, attributed to the research, is defensible.

### 2. When was the Northwestern photograph taken?

- **Known:** the catalogue gives only the collection's span. The print shows an "ILOJO HOTEL" sign, which is also in a photograph published in August 1946, and an "OJOBARO BROTHERS" sign, which is also in a photograph from the 1950s.
- **Inferred:** the later 1940s or the 1950s.
- **Open because:** signs can hang for decades, and I have not seen the back of the print.
- **What would close it:** a note on the back of print DW_12719; Duckworth's negative registers; the car's model, from a high-resolution scan; a directory entry for either business.

### 3. What was the building's footprint?

- **Known:** an L-shaped outline appears on a 1914 deed sketch and on a 2012 measured plan. The frontage to the square was 61 ft 1 in on the sketch.
- **Open because:** I have seen both only as a reproduction about 360 pixels wide, and may have misread a figure.
- **What would close it:** the 2012 measured drawings from Legacy or GHK Architects.
- **Decision, 10 October 2026:** the project goes ahead without them. The front's width comes from the 1914 sketch as I read it (about 18.6 m). The height (about 10 m), the depth and the rear are estimates and must be labelled as conjecture.

### 4. What does the plot look like now?

- **Known:** fenced rubble on 18 September 2016, from ten photographs. Open ground with stalls and tyres in February 2021, from one. A reconstruction was announced in January 2021. An August 2026 article says the state reclaimed the plot and put up a sign.
- **Open because:** two web searches and a search of Wikimedia Commons found no dated picture or description later than February 2021.
- **What would close it:** dated photographs of the corner of Alli Street and Bamgbose Street.

### 5. Do the two Northwestern links in the brief match the items I viewed?

- **Known:** I reached 34 items in the same collection through the library's public interface, including a print titled "Casa do Fernandez, on Tinubu Square, Lagos".
- **Open because:** the finding-aid pages in the brief would not open for me, so I cannot confirm the item numbers match.

### 6. What do the two Smithsonian photographs show?

- **Known:** nothing verified. A search summary describes one collection as an album of 1923 to 1925.
- **Open because:** both record pages would not open for me.
- **What would close it:** a person opening the links. This matters mostly for the Marina fallback.

### 7. When did the square change shape?

- **Known:** a triangle in 1913; a fenced park with a fountain by 2014.
- **Open because:** I found no dated plan between.
- **What would close it:** later survey sheets; the full-resolution aerials of 1948 and 1949.

### 8. What happened to the court, and when?

- **Known:** "Law Courts" on the 1913 plan; no court at the square on the 1962 map.
- **Unverified:** "demolished in 1960" (catalogue note); a building of 1918 and a first court of 1869 (other weak sources).
- **What would close it:** a dated photograph of the complete building; public works or judiciary records.

### 9. Where exactly did Dr Carr's house, the police station and the Methodist church stand?

- **Known:** the 1913 plan places "Police Constabulary" and a "Church". The catalogue says Carr's house was "just to the south of the Methodist Church".
- **Open because:** I could not tie the photographs to plots on a map, and I do not know whether the 1913 "Church" is the Methodist church of the photograph, or whether today's Church of the Trinity is on the same site.
- **What would close it:** a larger-scale plan with plot numbers; a photograph showing two of these buildings together with the square.

### 10. Are the poster dates right?

- **Known:** the cataloguer read four dates from posters.
- **Open because:** I could not confirm them at preview size.
- **What would close it:** full-resolution scans.

## Questions about use and rights

### 11. May the Northwestern photographs be used at all in the game?

- **Known:** they are in copyright and offered for research and scholarly use, with no redistribution.
- **My reading:** looking at them to inform a model is research use. Showing them, or putting them in the repository, is not.
- **Open because:** I am not a lawyer and no one has asked the library.
- **What would close it:** a written answer from the Herskovits Library. Whether a model closely based on one photograph needs permission is a question for them too.

### 12. Does share-alike reach a model made from the 2016 photographs?

- **Known:** the photographs are CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Open because:** whether a 3D model informed by photographs of a building is a derivative of those photographs is a legal question I cannot answer.
- **What would close it:** legal advice, or using the photographs only as reference alongside the developer's own.
